\section{Against the brief}

The original request set four conditions. All four are met; the table cites the
evidence rather than asserting it.

\begin{table}[H]
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{44mm}Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Requirement} & \textbf{Outcome} \\
\midrule
``Lightweight final model, not much compute for inferencing'' &
\code{feat\_logreg}: \textbf{3\,KB} of JSON weights, \textbf{0.14\,ms} on CPU,
one matrix multiply. \code{src/triage/} is \textbf{pure stdlib} --- no
scikit-learn, no numpy, no torch, no network at inference. \\
\addlinespace[3pt]
``Try other architectures too'' &
Seven scored on one split (\S3). Five reached 1.000; the smallest and fastest was
taken. The image CNN scored 0.792 / 0.492 at 6.1\,MB and 161\,ms. \\
\addlinespace[3pt]
``Over 75\% accuracy and equal or better precision'' &
\textbf{1.000 accuracy, 1.000 macro-precision}, against a bar of 0.75 and the
CNN's 0.492. \\
\addlinespace[3pt]
``Test generalizability \emph{before} creating a docker image'' &
Done first, on four axes (\S3): out-of-sample on 160 fresh unseen images with a
different label mix, repeated cross-validation, a learning curve, and an injected
EMERGENCY stress test. The image was built only afterwards. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\begin{callout}[stopRed]
\eyebrow{Read the 1.000 correctly}

\vspace{3pt}
The perfect scores mean the \emph{task} is deterministic, not that the model is
remarkable. On the out-of-sample set the 8B teacher's triage equalled the
classifier's own \code{referral\_priority} in \textbf{160 of 160 cases}, so the
deterministic rules, a 3\,KB linear model and an 8B LLM are indistinguishable
here. The value delivered is the removal of an LLM dependency --- with its
latency, cost and clinical-data egress --- from the decision path, not an
accuracy gain.
\end{callout}

\section{What was actually assessed}

The pipeline delegates two jobs to an external LLM. They are not the same problem
and cannot share a verdict: \textbf{triage} is a four-way classification over the
disease classifier's structured output; \textbf{narrative} is generation that no
classifier can produce.

Everything below rests on measurements taken against a live, self-hosted
Qwen3-8B-AWQ teacher on RTX A6000 hardware, not on estimates. Where a figure is
arithmetic rather than a measurement, it is labelled.

\section{Triage --- shipped}

A 2\,KB structured-feature logistic head reproduces the teacher's priority
exactly: accuracy and macro-precision \textbf{1.000} held-out, \textbf{1.000
$\pm$ 0.000} under repeated cross-validation, and \textbf{1.000} out-of-sample on
160 previously unseen images with a different label mix. The image CNN it
replaces scored 0.792 / 0.492 at 6.1\,MB and 161\,ms.

\begin{callout}
\eyebrow{Why it is this cheap}

\vspace{3pt}
On the out-of-sample set the teacher's triage equals the classifier's own
\code{referral\_priority} in \textbf{160 of 160 cases}. The 8B model adds nothing
to the triage \emph{decision}, so a 2\,KB linear model, the deterministic rules
and an 8B LLM all score identically. This is a cost elimination, not a modelling
triumph.
\end{callout}

\textbf{Shipped as:} JSON weights rather than a pickle --- the model is one matrix
multiply, so serving needs neither scikit-learn nor a pickle load at startup. The
exporter refuses to write weights that disagree with the fitted estimator on all
240 available cases, and the loader refuses an artifact whose feature layout has
drifted. An emergency code escalates through a deterministic override, since
EMERGENCY never appeared in training and the head cannot emit it.

\section{Narrative --- what shipped, and what it costs}

Two changes made the narrative half tractable.

\textbf{Prose-only output.} Every reliability defect measured was a
malformed-JSON defect, and the JSON existed only to carry the triage fields ---
which the 2\,KB head now serves. A narrator emitting plain prose has no
structured contract left to break. That is a stronger guarantee than
grammar-constrained decoding (which prevents \emph{invalid} JSON) and needs no
extra dependency.

\textbf{Vocabulary pruning.} bitsandbytes leaves the embedding table unquantized,
which was the entire size gap: 28.3M embedding params at bf16 (56.6\,MB) on top
of a 53.1\,MB 4-bit body predicts 110\,MB, and 109.8\,MB was measured. Pruning
the vocabulary to the tokens this task can reach --- \textbf{49{,}152 $\to$ 920}
--- collapses the embedding to under 1\,MB. It is done by id remapping rather
than tokenizer surgery, so it is exact and reversible.

\begin{callout}[stopRed]
\eyebrow{Two reasons the narrator ships disabled}

\vspace{3pt}
\textbf{4-bit trades a visible failure for an invisible one.} It clears the
60\,MB gate and always produces fluent text --- while dropping findings the
teacher reported in 42\% of cases. Fluent omission is harder to catch than a
blank output.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{bf16's clean scores do not generalise.} The audit set is dominated by
multi-finding cases resembling training data. Given a \emph{single}-finding case
by hand, bf16 produced ``a 75\% detection of diabetic retinopathy with a
prevalence of 88\%'' --- for one finding at 88\%. Both figures invented. One
probe is not a measurement, but it shows the 0.000 belongs to that split rather
than to the model.
\end{callout}

\subsection*{Why it is disabled on the deployed Space specifically}

The Space runs on free \code{cpu-basic} (2 vCPU). Measured there, throttled to
two threads:

\begin{table}[H]
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}Yrr@{}}
\toprule
& \textbf{Value} & \textbf{Against a \textasciitilde1\,s \code{/predict}} \\
\midrule
Model load                 & 8.6\,s    & once, at startup \\
Resident memory (fp32)     & 426.9\,MB & fp16 is emulated on CPU, so bf16's 213\,MB does not apply \\
Single-finding report      & 9.9\,s    & \textcolor{stopRed}{+10x latency} \\
Three-finding report       & 6.3\,s    & \textcolor{stopRed}{+6x latency} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

Two further constraints make it moot today. \code{transformers} is \textbf{not a
declared dependency} anywhere in \code{pyproject.toml}, so on the deployed image
the narrator import fails, the service catches it and serves the template ---
setting \code{NARRATOR\_ENABLED=true} would change nothing without an image
rebuild. And the small quantized builds \textbf{cannot run on CPU at all}:
bitsandbytes NF4/int8 kernels are CUDA-only, so the 54\,MB and 107\,MB variants
are unavailable on this tier. bf16 is the only option, at ten seconds a report.

\section{Deployment and live verification}

The platform is deployed to the Space
\code{landwind22/Retinal\_Screening} and is serving: \code{/health} returns
\code{model\_loaded: true} across all 45 diseases. It is the single deployment
target --- the previous Space is retired and now points here.

\textbf{The deploy pipeline used to lie.} It reported success as soon as the git
push landed, and twice pushed a Space that then failed to start --- once from a
clobbered README (\code{CONFIG\_ERROR} --- an over-length
\code{short\_description} had also kept the previous Space dark since 23 June),
once from a Dockerfile deleted by \code{rsync --delete} (\code{NO\_APP\_FILE}). A third deploy was silently dropped
when a push-triggered run and a manual dispatch raced to push. All three are
fixed: the workflow now polls the runtime stage and requires \code{/health} to
answer before passing, runs are serialised by a concurrency group, and a rejected
push refetches and retries. A 6-hourly keep-alive holds the free Space awake
against its 48\,h sleep timer.

\textbf{End-to-end regression} against the live deployment: \code{/health},
\code{/health/gate}, \code{/api/v1/diseases}, \code{/api/v1/predict/edge/status}
and \code{/docs} all 200 in \textasciitilde0.73\,s, and the fundus gate correctly
rejects a non-fundus image with \code{422 non\_fundus\_image}. The positive
prediction path could \emph{not} be exercised: every fundus image in
\code{data/} is a dangling symlink into a cleared cache, and the repository's own
fixture is random noise, which the gate is trained to reject.

\textbf{Mobile audit} (Lighthouse, Moto G Power emulation, throttled 4G --- the
mid-range Android this product targets):

Two defects, both real. \textbf{Text compression was off}: 481\,KB of 776\,KB
shipped uncompressed, costing 2.4\,s on 4G. The fix needed \code{gzip\_proxied
any} --- nginx reverse-proxies Next.js and uvicorn, and will not compress proxied
responses without it, which is exactly where the payload was. \textbf{Contrast}
failed on 31 nodes; the largest group was not a bad colour but
\code{opacity-70} blending a passing \code{slate-500} down to 2.71:1. Colour
values were recomputed per background --- on dark sections the correction runs
lighter, not darker, a distinction that cost one regression before it was caught.

Lighthouse scores move \textasciitilde5 points run to run on a shared runner; a
single number should not be read as a trend.

\section{Format evaluation --- AWQ, GPTQ, GGUF}

Rev.~1 recommended AWQ/GPTQ over bitsandbytes. Evaluating that properly reverses
the recommendation, for a reason specific to this model: a 135M SmolLM2 is
\textbf{21\% embedding}, and what each format does with that table decides the
footprint.

\begin{table}[H]
\caption{Projections are arithmetic from published quantization layouts, not
measurements --- none of the three could be run on this host.}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}YL{30mm}L{38mm}r@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Format} & \textbf{Body} & \textbf{Embedding} & \textbf{Total} \\
\midrule
bnb NF4 & 4-bit & fp16, not quantized & 110\,MB \\
bnb int8 & int8 & fp16, not quantized & 107\,MB \\
AWQ / GPTQ (W4A16) & 4-bit & fp16, conventionally excluded & $\sim$110\,MB \\
\rowcolor{softTeal}
\textbf{GGUF \code{Q4\_K\_M}} & $\sim$4.5-bit & \textbf{quantized too} & $\sim$\textbf{80\,MB} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\textbf{AWQ and GPTQ would not beat bitsandbytes on size here.} They exclude the
embedding exactly as bnb does, landing at the same $\sim$110\,MB; their advantage
is throughput via fused kernels, which does not address the binding constraint.
\textbf{GGUF is the only one of the three that quantizes the embedding}, making
\code{Q4\_K\_M}/\code{Q5\_K\_M} the most promising route into the 60--100\,MB band
\emph{without} vocabulary surgery --- and therefore without the 42\% omission
cost that pruning-plus-4-bit incurred.

\textbf{Pruning and standard formats are alternatives, not complements.} The
pruned checkpoint deliberately breaks the model/tokenizer correspondence
(\code{vocab\_size} 920 against a 49{,}152-token tokenizer, bridged by an explicit
id remap). GGUF bakes the tokenizer into the file, so conversion would emit
something whose ids do not mean what the weights expect; AWQ/GPTQ would run
mechanically but still need the custom wrapper, losing the vLLM/SGLang serving
compatibility that justifies adopting them.

\textbf{Tooling status (measured).} \code{autoawq} 0.2.9 installs but cannot
import against transformers 4.57.1 and is unmaintained upstream;
\code{gptqmodel} and \code{llmcompressor} require torch $\geq$ 2.8;
\code{compressed-tensors} 0.11.0 is present as a vLLM dependency; llama.cpp
binaries (\code{llama-quantize}, \code{convert\_hf\_to\_gguf.py}) are absent, and
the \code{gguf} Python library alone writes only F16, not K-quants.

\section{Recommendation}

\textbf{Triage: done and deployed.} It is validated, effectively free, removes an
LLM dependency from the decision path outright, and is live in all three images
with the Space verified serving.

\textbf{Narrative: keep the template as the default and leave
\code{NARRATOR\_ENABLED=false}.} The bf16 narrator is shipped and usable for
evaluation, but nothing has changed about the reason not to enable it: the
instrumentation still cannot distinguish a good narrator from a bad one, and in a
clinical screening context that is the thing that has to work first. The
off-distribution probe (\S3) is a concrete reminder that clean held-out numbers
are not evidence of general reliability.

\textbf{Next, in order:} (1) regenerate traces at scale with the disclosure
mandated --- it closes the statistical-power and compliance gaps together and the
teacher is free; (2) build a real faithfulness evaluation with clinician review;
(3) try GGUF \code{Q4\_K\_M} for the 60--100\,MB target rather than more
bitsandbytes; (4) measure under vLLM before quoting any latency. Only then
revisit enabling the narrator by default.

The honest summary is unchanged from Rev.~1: the expensive component turned out
to be unnecessary for triage, and not yet trustworthy for narrative. The first is
now banked.
